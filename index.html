About Us

Agriguardian Pvt Ltd

An agriculture technology company building intelligent monitoring systems for cardamom plantations — combining robotics, IoT, and cloud AI to make precision farming practical for every grower.

AgriGuardian is our flagship platform, purpose-built for the unique challenges of cardamom farming — dense canopy shade, scattered disease outbreaks, and labor-intensive manual scouting.

We partner with plantation owners, estate managers, and agronomy experts to deliver a complete monitoring loop: autonomous capture, secure cloud analysis, and instant farmer-facing recommendations.

Our Founding Story

Agriguardian was founded after seeing cardamom growers lose yield to diseases that were detected too late — often after weeks of manual inspection across difficult terrain. Traditional fixed cameras couldn't cover shaded plantations reliably, and soil health was checked only sporadically.

We set out to build a mobile, intelligent monitoring system that travels with the crop, senses the soil on demand, and puts actionable insights directly in farmers' hands.

Our Mission

Make advanced crop monitoring accessible for cardamom growers so they can protect yield, reduce waste, and farm with confidence.

Our Vision

A future where every plantation — regardless of size — has continuous, intelligent monitoring as a standard part of farming.

Our Approach

Farmer-first design, reliable hardware in tough field conditions, transparent AI predictions, and long-term partnerships with our customers.

Workflow

How AgriGuardian Works

Three simple steps — from field data to actionable insights for your plantation.

Step 01

Collect Data

The zipline robot captures high-resolution leaf images while a retractable sensor module measures soil moisture and environmental metrics at predefined points.

Step 02

AI Analysis

Images and sensor data are securely transmitted to our cloud AI model trained on cardamom diseases, producing predictions and confidence scores in near real time.

Step 03

Deliver Insights

Disease alerts, treatment recommendations, and soil health reports are delivered instantly to the AgriGuardian mobile app — so farmers can act before problems spread.

Autonomous Zipline Robot

Mobile monitoring replaces fixed cameras, delivering consistent leaf imaging across the plantation — even in dense, shaded cardamom environments.

Cloud AI Disease Model

A trained AI pipeline analyzes leaf imagery to identify cardamom diseases and returns predictions, confidence scores, and disease details in near real time.

Retractable Soil Monitoring

A motorized sensor module measures soil moisture and other field metrics at defined locations, then retracts — enabling accurate soil health insights without permanent installations.
